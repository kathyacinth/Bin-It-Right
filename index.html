Community Impact & Purpose

Why Proper Waste Segregation Matters to Our Community

Every piece of trash sorted accurately at home, in schools, and in local businesses creates a measurable ripple effect across our shared environment and public health.

Prevents Clogged Waterways

Separating non-biodegradable plastics from daily waste stops single-use wrappers and bottles from blocking barangay drainage canals, significantly reducing urban flooding during heavy rains.

Cuts Methane & Landfill Burden

When biodegradable food scraps are buried beneath plastics in landfills, they rot without oxygen and release methane—a greenhouse gas 28x more potent than CO₂. Composting turns waste into fertile soil.

Protects Waste Workers

Pre-segregating clean recyclables from wet organic waste protects sanitation workers and local Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs) from hazardous contamination and improves recycling recovery rates.

Empowers Youth & Schools

Accessible AI tools turn environmental education into an interactive habit, helping students, households, and community members make confident, split-second disposal decisions.

Quick Reference

Biodegradable vs. Non-Biodegradable

Understanding the biological lifecycle of everyday materials helps keep our communities clean and circular.

Green Bin • Organic

Biodegradable

Materials derived from plants or animals that microorganisms (bacteria and fungi) can naturally decompose into water, carbon dioxide, and nutrient-rich compost.

Decomposition Time 2 Weeks – 6 Months

Common Examples

  • Fruit & veggie peels
  • Leftover food scraps
  • Dry leaves & twigs
  • Uncoated paper & cardboard
  • Coffee grounds & eggshells
  • Cotton & natural fibers
Best Action: Compost at home or place in municipal organic collection.
Blue / Dry Bin • Synthetic

Non-Biodegradable

Synthetic, manufactured, or mineral-based items that resist natural biological breakdown, persisting in ecosystems for centuries unless recovered, reused, or recycled.

Decomposition Time 100 – 1,000+ Years

Common Examples

  • Plastic bottles & bags
  • Aluminum & tin cans
  • Glass jars & bottles
  • Styrofoam & sachets
  • E-waste & batteries
  • Synthetic nylon & polyester
Best Action: Rinse clean, keep dry, and send to local recycling centers.
